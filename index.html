Mailtropy

Understand what's filling your Gmail inbox.

Mailtropy is a free Chrome extension that helps you find frequent senders, subscription-like email, and email clutter without compromising privacy.

Start a scan to see sender frequency, domain distribution, and subscription signals. Mailtropy provides insights and guidance so you can decide what to clean up yourself in Gmail.

Read-only insights. Local processing.

With your permission, Mailtropy uses Google OAuth and the Gmail API to read message metadata. It requests message IDs, thread IDs, timestamps, label IDs, and only the From and List-Unsubscribe headers. It does not request email bodies, subjects, snippets, or attachments.

Analysis happens in your browser. Processed metadata and results stay in local extension storage. Mailtropy does not send Gmail data to a backend, sell it, or share it with third parties.

Mailtropy does not send, modify, archive, or delete email. Subscription signals are based on the presence of a List-Unsubscribe header; you perform cleanup manually in Gmail.

Read the Mailtropy Privacy Policy for details about access, storage, retention, and your controls.
